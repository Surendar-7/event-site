We turn school days into big days.

Our team started in Chennai with one inter-school quiz and a borrowed microphone. Today our educators, coordinators and sports coaches plan competitions that are fair, safe and loud with cheering.

We handle registrations, question papers, judges, staging, safety and prizes, so teachers can enjoy the day too. Our newspaper partnerships bring statewide coverage to every winner.
